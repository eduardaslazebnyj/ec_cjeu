Subject: Case No IV/35754 - Consortium East Africa Container Service (EACS)

Dear Sirs,

I have pleasure in informing you that the Commission has decided not to oppose your clients'
application in the case referred to above, which you notified, on 17 October 1995 pursuant to
Article 7 of the· Commission Regulation N" 870/95Y>

Your clients' consortium agreement, the East Africa Container Service, is therefore deemed
exempt from the prohibition in Article 85(1) of the EC Treaty and benefits, as from the date
of notification, from the block exemption granted by the Commission Regulation N" 870/95
through the opposition procedure provided in Article 7 of the said Regulation until the date
of expiry of the Regulation - ie. 21 April 2000.

I draw your attention to the provisions in Article 9 of Regulation N" 870/95 which states that
any consortium enjoying the benefit of the block exemption must comply with the conditions
and obligations stated therein and to the undertakings made in the notification by your clients
to continue to comply with those conditions and obligations during the life of the exemption.
