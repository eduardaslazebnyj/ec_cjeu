Opinion of the Advisory Committee on restrictive practices and dominant positions at its meeting on 19 January 2018 concerning the draft decision relating to Case AT.40220 — Qualcomm (Exclusivity Payments)
Rapporteur: Austria
(2018/C 269/13)
1.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission that the relevant product market is the worldwide merchant market of LTE chipsets as defined in the draft decision.
2.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission that Qualcomm held a dominant position in the worldwide market for LTE chipsets between 1 January 2011 and 31 December 2016.
3.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission that the payments granted by Qualcomm to Apple as described in the draft decision were exclusivity payments.
4.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission's analysis of the capability of Qualcomm’s exclusivity payments to have anti-competitive effects and with the Commission's conclusion that these exclusivity payments constitute an abuse of a dominant position.
5.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission that Qualcomm has not demonstrated that its exclusivity payments were counterbalanced or outweighed by advantages in terms of efficiency that also benefit the consumer.
6.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission's assessment as regards the duration of the infringement as described in the draft decision.
7.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission that it has jurisdiction to apply Article 102 TFEU and Article 54 EEA in the present case.
8.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission that the conduct covered by the draft decision has an appreciable effect on trade between Member States and between the Contracting Parties within the meaning of Article 102 TFEU and Article 54 EEA.
9.
The Advisory Committee recommends the publication of its opinion in the
Official Journal of the European Union
.
