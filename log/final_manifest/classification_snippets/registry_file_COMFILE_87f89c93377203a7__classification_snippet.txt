Subject: Case No IV/36.282 - VSA Notification

Further to the publication of a Notice in the Official Journal concerning the above case (OJ No C 185, 18.6.1997, p. 4), I am pleased to inform you that the Commission has decided not to raise serious doubts.

Accordingly, the arrangements falling within the scope of the above notification (as modified by the undertakings the parties have given to the Commission) will be deemed exempt for a period of six years for those activities falling within Regulation (EEC) No 4056/86 and for a period of three years for those activities falling within Regulation (EEC) No 1017/68.

These periods start to run as from 18 June 1997.
