Opinion of the Advisory Committee on Restrictive Practices and dominant position given at its meeting of 24 February 2014 regarding a draft decision relating to Case AT.39984 OPCOM/Romanian Power Exchange
Rapporteur: Hungary
2014/C 314/05
1.
The Advisory Committee shares the Commission’s concerns expressed in its draft Decision as communicated to the Advisory Committee.
A majority agrees and a minority disagrees.
2.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission’s definition of the relevant product market as the market for services facilitating short-term electricity trading.
3.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission that the relevant geographic market is the whole territory of Romania.
A majority agrees and a minority disagrees.
4.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission that OPCOM abused its dominant position on the relevant market by discriminating market participants on grounds of nationality/place of establishment without objective justification.
A majority agrees and a minority disagrees.
5.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission that Transelectrica should be held jointly and severally liable for the infringement of its subsidiary.
A majority agrees and a minority disagrees.
6.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission that a fine should be imposed on OPCOM and its parent company Transelectrica.
A majority agrees and a minority disagrees.
7.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission on the duration of the infringement to be taken into account for the calculation of the basic amount of the fine.
A majority agrees and a minority disagrees.
8.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission that there are no mitigating or aggravating circumstances to be taken into account.
A majority agrees and a minority disagrees.
9.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission on the final amount of the fine.
A majority agrees and a minority disagrees.
10.
The Advisory Committee recommends the publication of its opinion in the
Official Journal of the European Union
.
