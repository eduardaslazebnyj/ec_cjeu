SUBJECT: Case IV/35.807 - MEDECS

I refer to your notification dated 20 October 1995 by which you notify the MEDECS consortium agreement in accordance with Article 13 of Commission Regulation N° 870/95.

On the basis of the information provided on submission of your agreement and especially taking into account that you have informed that the parties' trade shares are 26.5% northbound and 23.0% southbound, which is below the 30% trade share limit for consortia which operates within a conference in Article 6(1) of the Regulation, the Commission's Directorate-General for Competition considers that this agreement appears to fulfil the conditions for benefitting from the block exemption for consortia under Commission Regulation (EC) No 870/95 of 20 April 1995 (Official Journal of the European Communities N° L 89 of 21 April 1995, p.7).

I would draw your attention to the provisions in Article 9 of Commission Regulation No 870/95; which states that any consortium enjoying the benefit of the Regulation must comply with the obligations stated therein. Those obligations must be strictly respected by your consortium.
