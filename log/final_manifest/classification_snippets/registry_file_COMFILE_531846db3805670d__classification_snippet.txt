Final report of the Hearing Officer
in case COMP/37.396/D2 – REVISED TACA

(pursuant to Article 15 of Commission Decision (2001/462/EC, ECSC)
of 23 May 2001 on the terms of reference of Hearing Officers
in certain competition proceedings – OJ L162, 19.06.2001, p.21)

On 29 January 1999, the following parties - Atlantic Container Line AB, Hapag-Lloyd
Container Line GmbH, Mediterranean Shipping Company SA, A.P. Moller – Maersk
Sealand, Nippon Yusen Kaisha, Orient Overseas Container Line Ltd and P & O Nedlloyd
Limited - notified an agreement (“Revised TACA”) to the Commission.

On 6 May 1999, pursuant to Article 12(2) of Regulation 4056/861 and to Article 12(2) of
Regulation 1017/682, the Commission published a summary of the application, inviting
comments from interested parties. Comments were received from a number of third
parties.

The Commission did not raise serious doubts as to the inland aspects of the Revised
TACA agreement within the 90 days period stipulated in Regulation 1017/68.
Accordingly these aspects of the agreement were deemed to be exempted, until 5 May
2002. On 3 May 2002 the parties requested a renewal of this exemption for all aspects of
the Revised TACA falling within the scope of the Regulation 1017/68. This will be
subject of another procedure and does not, accordingly, fall within the scope of the present
draft decision.

On 4 August 1999 i.e. within the stipulated 90 days period in Regulation No 4056/86, the
Commission sent a letter of serious doubts to the parties following which the Commission
continued its investigations into the maritime aspects of the Revised TACA agreement.

Final Report on the Hearing in Case N° COMP/37.396 – Revised TACA 2

On 1 December 2000 the parties submitted a supplementary notification amending their
agreement in certain specific respects. On 29 November 2001 the Commission published
a notice in the Official Journal pursuant to Article 23(3) of Regulation 4056/86 and Article
19(3) of Regulation 173 indicating it was minded to grant an exemption to the remaining
aspects of the Revised TACA. Comments were again received from third parties.

The draft decision grants an exemption under Article 81(3) to the remaining aspects of the
Revised TACA falling within the scope of Regulation 4056/86 and Regulation 17.

The rights of parties and third parties to be heard in the present case have been duly
respected.
