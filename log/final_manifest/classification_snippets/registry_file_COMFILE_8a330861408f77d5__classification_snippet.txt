# Case COMP/39736 SIEMENS / AREVA

# Commitments offered to the European Commission

# under Article 9 of Council Regulation N°1/2003

In accordance with Article 9 of Council Regulation N° 1/2003 ("Regulation 1/2003"), AREVA and SIEMENS offer the following commitments ("Commitments") to the European Commission (the "Commission") with a view to addressing the preliminary competition concerns as set out in the Commission's preliminary assessment ("Preliminary Assessment") dated 16 December 2011 in Case COMP/39736 and enabling the Commission to adopt a decision pursuant to Article 9 of Regulation 1/2003 confirming that the Commitments address its concerns ("Commitment decision").

Nothing in these Commitments may be construed as implying that AREVA and SIEMENS agree with the presentation of the facts set out in the Preliminary Assessment or with the preliminary competition concerns identified by the Commission therein. In addition, these Commitments may not be interpreted as an acknowledgement by AREVA or SIEMENS that they have infringed competition law.

Consistent with Article 9 of Regulation 1/2003, the Commitments are given in the understanding that the Commission will confirm that there are no grounds for further action and will close its investigation (Article 9 Decision).

## SECTION I: Definitions

Settlement Agreement: the agreement signed on September 22, 2011, pursuant to which AREVA and SIEMENS agreed to abrogate and set aside all post-contractual obligations under the SHA as well as the Arbitral Award to the extent it concerns such post-contractual obligations. The Settlement Agreement also provides for new non-compete and confidentiality obligations applicable to SIEMENS.

NCO : Non-Compete Obligation.

## SECTION II: The Commitments

AREVA and SIEMENS agree and undertake that, as from the entry into force of these Commitments, only the following non-compete and confidentiality obligations shall apply to SIEMENS:

1. SIEMENS and/or its affiliates shall not develop any activities directly related to the nuclear island of nuclear power plants as detailed in the Core Products and Services list attached to these Commitments as Annex 1. This NCO shall last until October 16, 2012.

2. Until October 16, 2012, SIEMENS commits not to acquire or hold more than 10% of the capital or voting shares of an entity carrying out activities included in the Core Products and Services list attached to these Commitments as Annex 1. SIEMENS is entitled to acquire or hold up to 10% of the capital or voting shares in such entities provided that such shareholding does not carry governance or management or special information rights.

3. SIEMENS and/or its affiliates shall be bound by confidentiality obligations as set out in Annex 2 to these Commitments until October 16, 2012 in relation to any information it may have had access to relating to the Core Products and Services.

4. Until such time, SIEMENS shall ensure by appropriate instructions and undertakings that its employees and advisors comply with such confidentiality obligations to avoid using or disclosing AREVA's confidential information.

5. In accordance with the principle of good faith and customary business practices, SIEMENS will not make available to third parties AREVA NP's corporate constitution and administration documents (including minutes of the governance bodies) or confidential written technical information, or use such technical information, even beyond October 16, 2012.

AREVA and SIEMENS herewith agree that any post contractual obligation contained in the Settlement Agreement which is not consistent with these Commitments shall be set aside.

Except as expressly provided in these Commitments, the Settlement Agreement shall remain unchanged and in full force and effect.

## SECTION III: Final provisions

The Commitments and the amendments to the Settlement Agreement as provided herein shall take effect upon the date of notification of the Article 9 Decision by which the Commission makes the Commitments binding on AREVA and SIEMENS.

The Annexes to this document forms an integral part of the Commitments.

## Annex 2 - Confidentiality

1. Each party undertakes not to disclose or use for its benefit or that of any third party any confidential information regarding AREVA NP or the other party (or those of its subsidiaries or affiliates) currently in their possession or which may have come into their possession during any due diligence exercise performed in connection with the creation of AREVA NP or during the lifetime of the joint venture.

2. Confidential information shall mean all information (whether visual and/or verbal) in respect of AREVA NP, the other party (and/or its subsidiaries, or affiliates) and its activities, having a technical, financial or commercial nature, particularly, without limitation, any information relating to the cost and price structure of the products manufactured and/or services rendered and sold by AREVA NP or the other party, together with any information relating to production processes, name of equipment, materials, parts, components, semi-products, suppliers, markets and customers.

3. This obligation shall also apply to proprietary or confidential data, information and know-how of each party which may have been exchanged in relation to the creation of AREVA NP.

4. The parties shall ensure to the maximum extent practicable that the content of AREVA NP's Shareholders Agreement shall remain confidential, unless otherwise legally required by a public authority or to defend their rights in a court of justice or arbitration, in which case prior written information shall be sent to the other party.

5. The parties shall take all necessary measures to ensure compliance herewith by their employees, representatives and agents.

6. The above obligations shall not apply to information already in the public domain.
