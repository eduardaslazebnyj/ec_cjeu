Official Journal of the European Union
C 140/9
Opinion of the Advisory Committee on restrictive agreements and dominant positions given at its meeting of 21 January 2013 regarding a draft decision relating to Case COMP/39.839 (2) — Telefónica and Portugal Telecom
Rapporteur: Latvia
2013/C 140/05
1.
The majority of the Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission on the basic amount of the fines. A minority abstains.
2.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission that there are no aggravating circumstances to be taken into account.
3.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission that the termination of the infringement on 4 February 2011, 16 days after the opening of proceedings by the Commission, should be taken into account as a mitigating circumstance.
4.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission that there are no mitigating circumstances other than the one indicated under point 3 to be taken into account.
5.
The majority of the Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission on the final amount of the fines. A minority abstains.
6.
The Advisory Committee asks the Commission to take into account any other points raised during the discussion.
7.
The Advisory Committee recommends the publication of its opinion in the
Official Journal of the European Union
.
