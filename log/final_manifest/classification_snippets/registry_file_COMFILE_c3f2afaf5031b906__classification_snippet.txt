## Subject:Case No IV/35.770 - Joint Operational Service Agreement

I refer to the above application for exemption pursuant to Articles 7 and 13 of Regulation 870/95.

I am pleased to inform you that on 0 5. 111. 1997 the Commission decided not to oppose exemption and that accordingly the agreement is exempt for the life of the Regulation, ie until 21 April 2000.
