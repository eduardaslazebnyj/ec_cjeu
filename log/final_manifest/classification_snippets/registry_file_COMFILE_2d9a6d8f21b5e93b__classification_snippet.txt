## Subject: Case COMP/D2/38.021 – European to Caribbean Consortium

This letter is addressed to you in your quality as representative of CMA-CGM SA, A.P. Møller Maersk Sealand, Marfret and Nordana Line.

I refer to the above application made on 13 December 2000 pursuant to Article 7 of Regulation 823/2000. The six months time limit for opposing the deemed exemption therefore expired on 14 June 2001.

The purpose of this letter is to inform you that the Commission did not oppose the deemed exemption of the agreements as notified pursuant to Article 7 of Regulation 823/2000 on or before 14 June 2001. Accordingly, the notified agreements are deemed exempt for the duration of Regulation 823/2000, i.e. until 25 April 2005.

Yours sincerely,

Eno MOAVERO MILANESI
