Opinion of the Advisory Committee on restrictive practices and dominant positions given at its meeting of 17 July 2009 concerning a draft decision in case COMP/39.396 – Calcium Carbide
2009/C 301/12
1.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission on the basic amounts of the fines.
2.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission on the increase of the basic amount due to aggravating circumstances.
3.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission on the amounts of reduction of the fine based on the 2006 Commission Notice on the non-imposition or reduction of fines in cartel cases.
4.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission on the final amounts of the fines.
5.
The Advisory Committee recommends the publication of its opinion in the
Official Journal of the European Union
.
