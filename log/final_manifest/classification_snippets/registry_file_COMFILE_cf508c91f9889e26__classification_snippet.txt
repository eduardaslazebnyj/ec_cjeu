Subject: Case No IV/35812: G & C Consortium Agreement.
Ref: Your notification of 20 October 1995 registered on 6 november1995

I refer to the above notification by which you claim on behalf of your clients the benefit of the block exemption pursuant to Articles 7(1) and 13 of Commission Regulation N° 870/95 of 20 April 1995, for the G & C Consortium Agreement Agreement.

Article 13, final paragraph, of Regulation N°870/95 provides, exceptionally, a favourable procedural regime for consortia existing at the date of entry into effect of the Regulation. As a result, consortia existing on that date which have a trade share higher than 50% but which fulfil the other conditions of the block exemption may, for a period of six months following the entry into force of the Regulation, also benefit from the provisions of Article 7. Article 7 of the Regulation provides that the benefit of the group exemption is available, through a simplified opposition procedure, to a consortium whose trade share exceeds the limits of 30 or 35% but is not above 50% of the direct trade provided it fulfils the other conditions contained in Regulation n° 870/95.

A consortium which has a trade share below 30 or 35%, (depending depending on whether it is a consortium operating within or outside a liner Conference) benefits, pursuant to Article 6 of the Regulation, automatically from the block exemption provided it fulfils the conditions and obligations set out in Article 5 to 9 of Regulation n° 870/95.

On the basis of the information provided in your notification, the trade share of the G&C agreement is 19%, ie below the threshold of 30 or 35% and the opposition procedure provided in Article 7 as extended by Article 13, final paragraph, of Regulation N°870/95 is therefore neither applicable nor, in your case, necessary for the application of the group exemption.

The Commission's Directorate-General for Competition has now completed its examination of the case on the basis of which it considers that this agreement fulfus the conditions for the exemption under Commission Regulation N° 870/95 and benefits consequently automatically from the block exemption until the date of expiry of the Regulation - 21 April 2000. Therefore no further action will be taken by the Commission in respect of the notified agreement provided the trade share remains below 35%.

I draw your attention to the provisions of Article 9 of the Commission Regulation n° 870/95, which states that any consortium enjoying the benefit of the group exemptionismation must comply with the obligations stated therein, and to the undertakings made in the notification by your clients to continue to comply with those conditions and obligations during the life of the exemption. I also draw your attention to Article 12 of the aforesaid Regulation, which empowers the Commission to withdraw the benefit of the block exemption if it subsequently finds that a consortium agreement nevertheless has anti-competitive effects which are incompatible with Article 85(3) or are prohibited by Article 86 of the Treaty.

Your agreement will therefore be dealt with by means of an administrative letter. Consequently the file will now be closed.

However, the case could be reconsidered if the factual or legal situation changes as regards any essential aspect of the agreement which affects the view which should be taken of it.

Naturally, any reopening of the file would be without prejudice to the legal consequences of the notification, particularly as regards the immunity from fines provided by Article 19(4) of Council Regulation n° 4056/86.
