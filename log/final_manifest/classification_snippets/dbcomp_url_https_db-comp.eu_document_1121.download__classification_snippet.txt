1. INTRODUCTION

(1) The addressees of this Decision are liable for a single and continuous infringement of
Article 101 of the Treaty in the sector of flexible polyurethane foam. The
infringement consisted in direct and indirect price coordination of flexible foam
products and covered a total of 10 Member States. It lasted from 26 October 2005
until 27 July 2010.
