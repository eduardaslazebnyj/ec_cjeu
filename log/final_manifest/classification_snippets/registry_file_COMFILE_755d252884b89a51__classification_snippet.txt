Opinion on restrictive practices and dominant positions at its meeting on 4 July 2022 concerning a draft decision in case AT.40305 - Network sharing - Czech Republic
Rapporteur: Italy
(Text with EEA relevance)
(2022/C 452/03)
(1)
The Advisory Committee (12 Member States) shares the Commission’s concerns expressed in its draft Decision under Article 101 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union and Article 53 of the EEA Agreement.
(2)
The Advisory Committee (12 Member States) agrees with the Commission that the proceedings can be concluded by means of a decision pursuant to Article 9(1) of Council Regulation (EC) No 1/2003, as described in the draft Decision.
(3)
The Advisory Committee (12 Member States) agrees with the Commission that the offered commitments are suitable, necessary and proportionate and should be made legally binding, as set out in the draft Decision. 11 Member States voted in favour. 1 Member State abstained.
(4)
The Advisory Committee (12 Member States) agrees with the Commission’s draft Decision that, in light of the offered commitments, there are no longer grounds for action by the Commission, without prejudice to Article 9(2) of Regulation (EC) No 1/2003. 11 Member States voted in favour. 1 Member State abstained.
(5)
The Advisory Committee (12 Member States) recommends the publication of its opinion in the
Official Journal of the European Union
.
