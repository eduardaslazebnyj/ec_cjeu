Dear Sirs,

Subject: Case No IV/35.681 -North Sea Liner Conference Agreement

I refer to your clients' application in the case referred above, which you notified, on 16 August
1995, pursuant to Article 12 of Council Regulation N" 4056/86.

Further to the publication of a Notice in the Official Journal concerning the above case<1>, I
am pleased to inform you that the Commission has decided not to raise serious doubts and
accordingly allow the agreement to be deemed exempt for the time already elapsed and for
a period of six years for those activities falling within Regulation N" 4056/86 and for a period
of three years for those activities falling within Regulation N" 1017/68. In accordance with
Article 12(3) of Council Regulation N" 4056/86 and of Council Regulation N" 1017/68, those
periods will start to run as from 19 October 1996, the date of publication of the Notice in the
Official Journal .

The Commission's reasons for believing that the conditions of Article 85(3) are fulfilled in this
case are those summarised in the published Notice .
