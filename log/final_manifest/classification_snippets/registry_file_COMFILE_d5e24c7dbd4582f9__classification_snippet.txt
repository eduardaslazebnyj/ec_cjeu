Opinion of the Advisory Committee on restrictive practices and dominant positions at its meeting on 12 December 2022 concerning a draft decision in case AT.40462 - Amazon Marketplace and AT.40703 - Amazon Buy Box
Rapporteur: Bulgaria
(Text with EEA relevance)
(2023/C 87/06)
1.
The Advisory Committee (17 Member States) shares the Commission’s preliminary concerns expressed in its draft Decision under Article 102 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union and Article 54 of the EEA Agreement.
2.
The Advisory Committee (17 Member States) agrees with the Commission that the proceedings in the two cases AT.40703 and AT.40462 can be concluded by means of a decision pursuant to Article 9(1) of Council Regulation (EC) No 1/2003, as described in the draft Decision.
3.
The Advisory Committee (17 Member States) agrees with the Commission that the offered commitments are suitable, necessary and proportionate and should be made legally binding, as set out in the draft Decision.
4.
The Advisory Committee (17 Member States) agrees with the Commission’s draft Decision that, in light of the offered commitments, there are no longer grounds for action by the Commission, without prejudice to Article 9(2) of Regulation (EC) No 1/2003.
5.
The Advisory Committee recommends the publication of its opinion in the Official Journal.
6.
The Advisory Committee asks the Commission to take into account all the other points raised during the discussion.
