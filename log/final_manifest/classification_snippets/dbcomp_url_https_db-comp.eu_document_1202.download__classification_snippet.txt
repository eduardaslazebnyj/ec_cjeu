On 23 June 2010, the Commission adopted a decision relating to a proceeding under Article 101 of the Treaty on the
Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’). In accordance with the provisions of Article 30 of Council Regulation
(EC) No 1/2003 ( 1 ), the Commission herewith publishes the names of the parties and the main content of the decision,
including any penalties imposed, having regard to the legitimate interest of undertakings in the protection of their
business secrets. A non-confidential version of the decision will be available on the Directorate-General for Competition
website at:

http://ec.europa.eu/competition/antitrust/cases
