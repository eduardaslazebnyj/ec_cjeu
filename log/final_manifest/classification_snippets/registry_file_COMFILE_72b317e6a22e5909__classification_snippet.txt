CASE AT.40305 – CZECH NETWORK SHARING

COMMITMENTS OFFERED TO THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION
PURSUANT TO ARTICLE 9 OF THE COUNCIL REGULATION (EC) NO 1/2003

1 INTRODUCTION

1.1 In accordance with Article 9 of Council Regulation (EC) No 1/2003 (the ‘Regulation 1/2003’),
CETIN a.s. (‘CETIN’) and O2 Czech Republic a.s. (‘O2’; CETIN and O2 jointly also
as the ‘Committing Parties’) offer to the European Commission (the ‘Commission’)
in response to the Commission's concerns against CETIN, O2 and PPF Group N.V.
(‘PPFGNV’) in Case AT.40305 – Czech Network Sharing (the ‘Proceedings’) the following
commitment. The commitment concerns the Mobile Network Services Agreement of 2 June
2015 between CETIN and O2, as amended (the ‘MNSA’ and the commitment hereunder
as the ‘MNSA Commitment’).

1.2 On 25 October 2016, the Commission formally opened the Proceedings against CETIN, O2
and T-Mobile Czech Republic (‘TMCZ’) regarding the network sharing agreements between
TMCZ and CETIN on 2G, 3G and 4G technology, namely:

• the network sharing agreement of 29 October 2013 between TMCZ and CETIN (together
the ‘Sharing Parties’), as amended, covering 2G and 3G (the ‘2G/3G NSA’); and

• the network sharing agreement of 2 May 2014 between the Sharing Parties, as amended
(the ‘4G NSA’), covering 4G

(the 2G/3G NSA and the 4G NSA jointly also as the ‘NSAs’ and each individua lly
as the ‘NSA’).

1.3 On 7 August 2019, the Commission addressed the Statement of Objections No. C(2019) 6021
final to TMCZ, CETIN and O2 and, on 14 February 2020, the Statement of Objections
No. C(2020)961 final to PPFGNV and Deutsche Telekom AG (both Statements of Objections
collectively referred to as the ‘SO’). On 30 August 2021, the Commission then addressed
the Preliminary Assessment No. C(2021) 6483 final to CETIN, O2, PPFGNV, TMCZ
and Deutsche Telekom AG (the “PA”).

1.4 The MNSA Commitment is being made by the Committing Parties as a part of the other
commitments made by the Committing Parties (or one of them) and TMCZ within
the Proceedings (the “Other Commitments”). The Committing Parties offer the MNSA
Commitment along with the Other Commitments to meet the Commission’s concerns on the
understanding that the Commission will adopt a decision pursuant to Article 9 of Council
Regulation 1/2003 making the MNSA Commitment binding, concluding that there are
no grounds for further action against CETIN, O2 and PPFGNV or any of their subsidiaries, and
closing the Proceedings (the ‘Commitment Decision’).
1

1.5 Consistent with Article 9 of Regulation 1/2003, the MNSA Commitment is made without
prejudice to CETIN's and O2’s position that the NSAs do not infringe Article 101 TFEU or any
other competition law or other laws of the European Union or its Member States and that the
MNSA also does not raise or exacerbate any competition concerns. The MNSA Commitment
cannot be interpreted as an acknowledgement by CETIN and/or O2 that any of the allegations
made by the Commission in the SO, the PA and/or elsewhere within the Proceedings are valid.

1.6 The capitalised terms in this MNSA Commitment shall have the meaning ascribed to them in
the text of this MNSA Commitment as well as, for the avoidance of doubts, in Section 7.

2 AMENDMENT TO THE MOBILE NETWORK SERVICES AGREEMENT
BETWEEN CETIN AND O2

2.1 Within 2 months as of the date on which O2 and CETIN are both notified by the Commission
of the adoption of the final Commitment Decision (the “Commencement Date”), CETIN and
O2 will modify the MNSA as described below.

2.2 The MNSA Commitment shall ensure that the intended meaning behind the MNSA Clauses
concerned by the MNSA Commitment is clear and that no doubts arise as to the interpretation
of their impact on the relations between CETIN and O2 and/or on the treatment of O2 and
TMCZ, respectively, by CETIN, all of the foregoing in relation to the position of CETIN, O2
and TMCZ in relation to the mobile network operated by CETIN under the MNSA and the
NSAs.

2.3 The contractual modifications shall concern the specification and clarification concerning the
following areas:

a) CETIN shall treat (i) O2 under the MNSA [provisions of MNSA related to network
deployment] to (ii) TMCZ under the NSAs solely to the extent of the so-called
[provisions of MNSA related to network deployment] services, i.e. the services which
CETIN and TMCZ provide to each other [provisions of MNSA related to network
deployment] under the NSAs [provisions of MNSA related to network deployment]
whereas, for the avoidance of doubts, such services exclude any individual network
enhancements, upgrades etc. which services are to be requested from CETIN
individually and independently of each other by each of O2 and TMCZ, if desired (the
“Network Enhancements”). In other words, the [provisions of MNSA related to
network deployment] treatment shall only apply in order to ensure that the [provisions
of MNSA related to network deployment] operation and maintenance of the mobile
network provided by CETIN and TMCZ (in the positions of the respective master
operators) to each other is extended also to O2 (in a position of a retail-only mobile
network operator without its own mobile network infrastructure).

b) CETIN shall not (i) inform O2 about or (ii) pass on or otherwise automatically make
available to O2 [provisions of MNSA related to information exchange].

2

c) CETIN shall not be constrained by O2 in changing the design and composition of the
mobile network [provisions of MNSA related to network changes].

2.4 The foregoing shall be, for the avoidance of any doubts, articulated as generally applicable
principles in Clauses 4.2, 4.4, 4.5 and 4.7 of the MNSA.

2.5 The particular contractual modifications of the MNSA subject to the MNSA Commitment are
specified in Annex 1 hereto.

3 DURATION OF MNSA COMMITMENT

3.1 The MNSA Commitment will take effect on and from the Commencement Date.

3.2 Without prejudice to Article 9(2) of Council Regulation 1/2003 the MNSA Commitment will
remain in force for a period of (i) the term of the MNSA or (ii) the term of the NSAs whichever
of those terms ends earlier. It is understood that the MNSA Commitment will remain in force
in case that any of the NSAs is terminated while the other NSA remains valid and in force.

4 MONITORING TRUSTEE

4.1 Given the scope of the MNSA Commitment, no monitoring by a monitoring trustee is
envisaged. The compliance with the MNSA Commitment will be proved to the Commission by
delivering copies of the signed contractual documents containing the MNSA modification as
described in Annex 1 herein.

5 REVIEW CLAUSE

5.1 Without prejudice to the general provision of Article 9(2) of Council Regulation 1/2003, the
Committing Parties may request the Commission to review and modify the MNSA
Commitment where there has been a material change in any of the relevant facts, law or
circumstances on which the Commitment Decision was based.

5.2 Without prejudice to the provisions of Article 9(2) of Council Regulation 1/2003, the
Commission may, on its own initiative, review the MNSA Commitment if there has been a
material change in any of the facts on which the Commitment Decision was based.

6 NO CIRCUMVENTION

6.1 Without prejudice to the general provision of Article 9(2) of Council Regulation 1/2003, the
Committing Parties shall not circumvent or attempt to circumvent the MNSA Commitment in
any way. For the avoidance of doubt, the mere fact that a network sharing agreement is
concluded with regards to the 5G technology shall not constitute circumvention of the MNSA
Commitment.
