Official Journal of the European Union
C 83/5
Opinion of the Advisory Committee on restrictive practices and dominant positions given at its 428th meeting on 29 June 2007 concerning a preliminary draft decision relating to Case COMP/38.784 — Wanadoo España v Telefónica
Rapporteur: United Kingdom
(2008/C 83/04)
1.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission's proposal to impose a fine. A minority abstains.
2.
The Advisory Committee agrees that the fine should be in the very serious range. A minority disagrees.
3.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission's proposal to reduce the fine under mitigating circumstances to take account of some of Telefónica's charges being subject to sector specific regulation by the Spanish CMT.
4.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the final amount of fine. A minority abstains and disagrees.
5.
The Advisory Committee agrees that its opinion should be published.
6.
The Advisory Committee asks the Commission to take into account all the other points raised during the discussion.
