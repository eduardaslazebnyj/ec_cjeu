## Subject: Case No IV/35.680 - Baltic Liner Conference Agreement

Further to the publication of a Notice in the Official Journal concerning the above case $^{(1)}$ , I am pleased to inform you that the Commission has decided not to raise serious doubts and accordingly allow the agreement to be deemed exempt for a period of six years for those activities falling within Regulation 4056/86 and for a period of three years for those activities falling within Regulation 1017/68. Those periods will start to run as from 23 August 1995.

The Commission's reasons for believing that the conditions of Article 85(3) are fulfilled in this case are those summarised in the published Notice. In particular, we believe that weight should be given to the fact that the cooperation in question has increased the efficiency of the parties' operations and has enabled them to undertake significant investment in equipment particularly suited to the climatic conditions in the Galfs of Bothnia and Finland and to the special needs of Finnish shippers. The parties' share of the relevant market does not appear to be such as to allow them to eliminate competition for a substantial part of the services in question. The Commission has also given weight to the declaration that the Conference intends to comply with the conditions attached to the group exemption for liner conferences contained in Regulation (EEC) No 4056/86 in their most important respects.
