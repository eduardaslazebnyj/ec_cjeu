CASE AT.40305 – Network sharing - Czech Republic

COMMITMENTS OFFERED TO THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION
PURSUANT TO ARTICLE 9 OF COUNCIL REGULATION (EC) NO 1/2003

1 INTRODUCTION

1.1 In accordance with Article 9 of Council Regulation (EC) No 1/2003 (Regulation 1/2003),
CETIN a.s., formerly Česká telekomunikační infrastruktura a.s. (“CETIN”) offers the
following commitments (“Commitments”) to the European Commission (“Commission”)
in full response to the Commission's concerns against CETIN and PPF Group N.V.
(“PPFGNV”) in Case AT.40305 - Network sharing - Czech Republic.

1.2 On 25 October 2016, the Commission opened formal proceedings against T-Mobile Czech
Republic a.s. (“TMCZ”), CETIN and O2 Czech Republic a.s. (“O2”) regarding the
network sharing agreements between TMCZ and CETIN on 2G, 3G and 4G technology
(“Investigation”), namely:

 The network sharing agreement of 29 October 2013 between TMCZ and CETIN
(together “Parties”) covering 2G and 3G (“2G/3G NSA”); and

 The network sharing agreement of 2 May 2014 between the Parties covering 4G (“4G
NSA”) (the 2G/3G NSA and the 4G NSA are together referred to as the “NSAs”).

1.3 On 7 August 2019, the Commission addressed the Statement of Objections No. C(2019)
6021 final to TMCZ, CETIN and O2 and, on 14 February 2020, the Statement of
Objections No. C(2020)961 final to PPFGNV and Deutsche Telekom AG (“DT”) (both
Statements of Objections collectively referred to as the “SO”). Following the responses of
the Parties and the Oral Hearing held in September 2020, the Commission reviewed its
concerns in the Preliminary Assessment (“PA”) C(2021) 6483 final addressed to CETIN,
O2, PPFGNV,TMCZ and DT on 30 August 2021.

1.4 CETIN offers the Commitments to meet the Commission’s concerns on the understanding
that the Commission will adopt a decision pursuant to Article 9 of Council Regulation
1/2003 making the Commitments binding, concluding that there are no grounds for further
action against CETIN, PPFGNV or any of their subsidiaries, and closing the Investigation
(the “Commitment Decision”).

1.5 The Commitments are conditional upon the Commission making substantially the same
commitments binding on TMCZ.

1.6 Consistent with Article 9 of Regulation 1/2003, these Commitments, which relate only to
the NSAs, are made without prejudice to CETIN's position that its NSAs do not infringe
Article 101 TFEU or any other competition law or other laws of the European Union or its
Member States. The Commitments cannot be interpreted as an acknowledgement by
CETIN that any of the allegations made in the SO and/or the PA are valid.

2 NETWORK MODERNIZATION COMMITMENT: PROVIDE MULTI STANDARD
RAN EQUIPMENT IN THE MID BAND LAYERS (1800MHZ AND 2100MHZ).

2.1 CETIN will implement multi standard RAN equipment in the Mid Band layers (1800 MHz
and 2100 MHz) of its master area of the shared network (“Master Area”) which allows
each Party to independently decide which radio access technology (including 4G and 5G) it
wants to use on each frequency within the Mid Bands. With this commitment, each Party is
able to speed up roll-out and refarming of Mid Bands spectrum to more efficient
technologies, in particular 5G. This will allow the individual increase of LTE and 5G usage
in the Mid Bands according to the Party specific needs.

2.2 This commitment allows the Parties to deploy both band layers (1800 MHz and 2100MHz)
on all existing sites where, by the end of March 2021, at least one Mid Band layer has been
active, i.e., [1500-2000] sites in the CETIN Master Area (as identified in Annex 1),
resulting in overall increased LTE capacity and a faster 5G roll-out for the CZ consumers.

2.3 To achieve the above commitment, CETIN will replace existing RAN equipment
(“Existing RAN Equipment”) in CETIN Master Area on sites where 1800MHz and/or
2100MHz has been already installed by the end of March 2021, including both hardware
(“HW”) and software (“SW”), in the Mid Bands (1800MHz and 2100MHz) by multistandard RAN equipment (“the Network Modernization Commitment”).

2.4 The Network Modernization Commitment will occur within [less than 5 years] of the
Commencement Date in accordance with the below roll-out plan. As from the
Commencement Date, any new deployment in the Mid Bands will be multi-standard
capable.

2.5 The Network Modernization Commitment would give each Party the full flexibility to
individually decide how, where and when to deploy or change or activate a specific access
technology, as it suits the Party ́s business needs, without being dependent on additional
network changes or HW changes required by the other Party. Such a commitment also
ensures that each Party benefits from full freedom in the deployment of new technologies,
in particular with regard to the ability to refarm spectrum for new technologies. This would
substantially increase each operator ́s flexibility for capacity build out and facilitate a faster
5G capacity roll-out on the Mid Bands.

3 SETTING AND REVIEW OF THE FINANCIAL CONDITIONS FOR
UNILATERAL DEPLOYMENTS

3.1 CETIN will modify the financial conditions for Unilateral Deployments to follow a costbased principle. Accordingly, within 3 months of the Commencement Date, the Price List
for Unilateral Deployments will be reviewed so that prices for Unilateral Deployments are
based on incremental costs (“Incremental Costs”), with no margin for overheads. For the
avoidance of doubt, the applicable prices for each type of Unilateral Deployment will be
the same for both Parties (“Single Pricing”). The applicable prices under the Single Pricing
will be [cost information] Incremental Costs of each Party for the relevant type of
Unilateral Deployment.

3.2 To the above effect, each Party will deliver to the Monitoring Trustee its proposal of the
Price List to be applied in respect of each type of Unilateral Deployments, together with the
calculations of the related costs and relevant background materials. The joint Price List will
then be set-up by the Monitoring Trustee, within one month from receiving the proposal of
both Parties, on the basis of the principle set out in clause 3.1. (i.e., Single Pricing and [cost
information] the Incremental Costs of each Party). For the avoidance of doubts, the
Incremental Costs calculations (as well as the related information and background
materials) of the Parties shall never be exchanged between the Parties. In case of
disagreement with the price provided by either Party, the Monitoring Trustee will inform
such Party in writing of the reasons for the disagreement and the proposed alternative price
and give it the opportunity to respond orally and in writing. If the disagreement persists, the
Monitoring Trustee will report to the Commission in accordance with paragraph 6.8 (f).

3.3 The Price List will be applicable within one month of the confirmation by the Monitoring
Trustee. Subsequently, the Parties to the NSAs will assess the necessity to review the Price
List on a [...] basis, aligned with the potential changes in Incremental Costs. To that end,
each Party will follow the same procedure as under paragraph 3.2.

4 IMPROVEMENT OF THE NSAS CONTRACTUAL PROVISIONS AS REGARDS
INFORMATION EXCHANGE

4.1 Within 2 months of the Commencement Date, CETIN will agree to modify the NSAs to
further limit the information exchange between the Parties in the following manner.

Streamline the governance structure:

 CETIN will streamline governance level committees and meetings, specifically by:

o Removing the strategic level (including steering committee) from the governance
and collaboration model to keep only the tactical level and operational level, and

o Adapting existing contacts lists and roles in the NSAs accordingly.

 CETIN will implement additional ring-fencing between workgroups, by:

3

o Limiting workgroups to technical staff only; making as a general rule that nontechnical staff are prohibited from attending meetings discussing specific technical
solutions; meetings involving non-technical staff will only take place to the extent
objectively necessary and under defined rules for their participation.

o Streamlining and possibly reducing the number of meetings of workgroups so that
it is strictly limited to a minimum number necessary to ensure NSAs operation.

o Simplifying the current description of the management structure and
responsibilities: Parties to be represented in principle by technicians delegated to
the respective technical workgroups; structure with different levels of management
to be streamlined.

o For situations where the input of non-technical staff is essential, organizing
meetings with strictly specified and restricted agenda and scope of information.
scrutiny by in-house compliance officer and/or in-house legal counsel will be
required for such meetings. Involvement of certain non-technical staff (such as
marketing staff) will be prohibited in any case.

o Inserting a mechanism ensuring that the workgroups are staffed on a need-to-know
basis.

 CETIN will provide further details as to the information flows specific to workgroups, by:

o Updating and refining the list of categories of information that can particularly be
exchanged within each respective workgroup based on the principle that the
information exchanged should be limited to the specific tasks/subject-areas dealt
with by each workgroup. Other workgroups, dealing with other agendas, should
have access to different sets of information.

o Strictly limiting access to documents to the members of a respective workgroup.

o Specifying in a greater detail what NSA information may be handed out to the
non-technical staff and setting pre-defined communication filters (Chinese walls).
The information communicated should be limited to the facts necessary for
development / innovations of the respective operator’s own network and should be
shared, to the extent feasible, in aggregated format.

o Classifying Commercially Sensitive Information (defined in the NSAs) as
Prohibited Information which cannot be exchanged at all.

o Inserting provisions mandating monitoring of compliance with the rules on
exchange of information by a Monitoring Trustee for a pre-defined time period.

4

Update or remove NSAs provisions referring to specific information exchanges:

 Removing the reference to the possibility of potential cooperation in the area of spectrum
sharing and add references to the applicable competition guidelines and information
exchange rules.

 Removing the obligation to annually discuss changes of [information about the setup of
the NSAs] and replacing it with discussions on a need-to-know basis (under specified
conditions, e.g. in case of [information about the setup of the NSAs], as an evaluation of
aggregate data).

 Streamlining or adapting the clause concerning [information about the setup of the
NSAs].

No exchange of traffic and capacity forecasts:

 CETIN will explicitly prohibit the exchange of capacity forecasts (i.e., information on
expected number of installations of new services in a particular period) and traffic
forecasts by classifying these as “Prohibited Information”, and remove any related
references in the NSA.

4.2 The specific modifications to the wording to the 4G NSA and the 2G/3G NSA are attached
in Annex 2.

Reflecting the separation of CETIN and O2

 The changes will reflect the separation of O2 and CETIN that took place after the
conclusion of the NSAs, recognizing in particular that CETIN does not reveal to any of
the MNOs any information concerning individual upgrades for any of the MNOs unless
objectively necessary.

5 NO EXTENSION OF THE NSAS TO PRAGUE AND BRNO

5.1 Unless mandated by public authority or regulation, CETIN commits to continue not to
extend its NSAs with TMCZ to cover [...] macro deployments in the frequency- bands [...]
in Prague and Brno (the “Prague and Brno Commitment”). For the avoidance of doubt,
the Prague and Brno Commitment does not alter CETIN’s ability to maintain or conclude
[...] agreements, or ad-hoc sharing agreements in relation to specific (i) locations such as
metro undergrounds, tunnels, transport corridors and historic areas, or (ii) solutions such as
dedicated B2B solutions, with TMCZ and/or any other operator.

5.2 The Prague and Brno Commitment will apply until [end date within 7-10 years].

6 MONITORING TRUSTEE

6.1 CETIN and TMCZ shall appoint a Monitoring Trustee to monitor CETIN's compliance
with these Commitments.

5

6.2 The Trustee shall be independent of CETIN or any other undertaking directly or indirectly
controlled or affiliated with CETIN and shall possess the necessary qualifications to carry
out its mandate and shall neither have nor become exposed to any conflict of interest. In
particular, members of the Trustee team shall not provide services to or become employees
of CETIN or its affiliates neither during the Trustee mandate nor for a period of three years
following termination of the Trustee mandate. The Trustee shall be remunerated equally by
CETIN and TMCZ in a way that does not impede the independent and effective fulfilment
of its mandate.

Proposal by the Parties

6.3 No later than one week after the Commencement Date, CETIN and TMCZ will submit a
list of at least two persons whom they propose to appoint as the Monitoring Trustee to the
Commission for approval. The proposal shall contain sufficient information for the
Commission to verify that the proposed Trustee fulfils the requirements set out in
paragraph 6.2 above and shall include:

(a) the full terms of the proposed mandate, which shall include all provisions necessary
to enable the Trustee to fulfil its duties under these Commitments; and

(b) the outline of a plan which describes how the Trustee intends to carry out its
assigned tasks.

Approval or rejection by the Commission

6.4 The Commission shall have the discretion to approve or reject the proposed Trustee(s) and
to approve the proposed mandate subject to any modifications it deems necessary for the
Trustee to fulfil its obligations. If only one name is approved, CETIN shall appoint or cause
to be appointed, the individual or institution concerned as Trustee, in accordance with the
mandate approved by the Commission. If more than one name is approved, CETIN and
TMCZ shall be free to choose the Trustee to be appointed from among the names approved.
The Trustee shall be appointed within one week of the Commission's approval, in
accordance with the mandate approved by the Commission.

New proposal by the Parties

Duties and Obligations on TMCZ

6.9 TMCZ shall provide and cause its advisors to provide the Monitoring Trustee with all such
co-operation, assistance and information, as the Monitoring Trustee may reasonably require
to perform its tasks. The Trustee shall have full and complete access, subject to reasonable
notice, to any of TMCZ's books, records, documents, management or other personnel,
facilities, sites and technical information, save for legally privileged material, necessary for
fulfilling its duties under the Commitments, and TMCZ shall provide the Trustee upon
request with copies of any such document. TMCZ shall make available to the Monitoring
Trustee one or more offices on their premises and shall be available for meetings in order to
provide the Trustee with all information that is reasonably necessary for the performance of
its tasks.

6.10 TMCZ and CETIN shall, on an equal basis, indemnify the Monitoring Trustee and its
employees and agents (each an “Indemnified Party”) and hold each Indemnified Party
harmless against, and hereby agrees that an Indemnified Party shall have no liability to
TMCZ for any liabilities arising out of the performance of the Monitoring Trustee’s duties
under the Commitments, except to the extent that such liabilities result from the willful
default, recklessness, negligence or bad faith of the Monitoring Trustee, its employees,
agents or advisors.

Replacement, discharge and reappointment of the Trustee

6.11 If the Trustee ceases to perform its functions under the Commitments or for any other good
cause, including the exposure of the Trustee to a conflict of interest:

(a) the Commission may, after hearing the Trustee, require TMCZ and CETIN to
replace the Trustee; or

(b) TMCZ and CETIN, with the prior approval of the Commission, may replace the
Trustee.

6.12 If the Trustee is removed, the Trustee may be required to continue in its function until a
new Trustee is in place to whom the Trustee has effected a full hand over of all relevant
information. The new Trustee shall be appointed in accordance with the procedure referred
to above.

6.13 Beside the removal according to clause 6.11, the Trustee shall cease to act as Trustee only
after the Commission has discharged it from its duties after all the Commitments with
which the Trustee has been entrusted have been implemented. However, the Commission
may at any time require the reappointment of the Trustee if it subsequently appears that the
relevant commitments might not have been fully and properly implemented.

8 REVIEW CLAUSE

8.1 Without prejudice to the general provision of Article 9(2) of Council Regulation 1/2003,
TMCZ may request the Commission to review and modify the Commitments where there
has been a material change in any of the relevant facts, law or circumstances on which the
Commission's Decision pursuant to Article 9(1) of Council Regulation 1/2003 was based.
For the avoidance of doubt, any modification or termination by CETIN of its own
commitments towards the Commission or any non-fulfillment thereof as well as any early
termination of any of the NSAs will be considered as a material change.

8.2 Without prejudice to the provisions of Article 9(2) of Council Regulation 1/2003, the
Commission may, on its own initiative, review these Commitments if there has been a
material change in any of the facts on which the Commission's Decision pursuant to Article
9(1) of Council Regulation 1/2003 was based.

9 NO CIRCUMVENTION

9.1 Without prejudice to the general provision of Article 9(2) of Council Regulation 1/2003,
TMCZ shall not circumvent or attempt to circumvent these Commitments in any way. For
the avoidance of doubt, the mere fact that a network sharing agreement is concluded with
regards to the 5G technology shall not constitute circumvention of the Commitments.
