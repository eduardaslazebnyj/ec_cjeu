# Registered with acknowledgement of receipt

Subject: Case No IV/35762 - Consortium Joint Mediterranean Canada Service (JMCS)

Dear Sirs,

I have pleasure in informing you that the Commission has decided not to oppose your clients' application in the case referred to above, which you notified, on 19 October 1995 pursuant to Article 7 of the Commission Regulation N° 870/95 $^{(1)}$ .

Your clients' consortium agreement, the Joint Mediterranean Canada Service, is therefore deemed exempt from the prohibition in Article 85(1) of the EC Treaty and benefits, as from the date of notification, from the block exemption granted by the Commission Regulation N° 870/95 through the opposition procedure provided in Article 7 as extended by Article 13 of the same Regulation until the date of expiry of the Regulation - ie. 21 April 2000.

I draw your attention to the provisions in Article 9 of Regulation N° 870/95 which states that any consortium enjoying the benefit of the block exemption must comply with the conditions and obligations stated therein and to the undertakings made in the notification by your clients to continue to comply with those conditions and obligations during the life of the exemption.

Yours faithfully,
