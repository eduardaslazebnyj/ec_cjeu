SUBJECT: CASE No IV/35808 - MEDSAP Consortium
Ref: Your letter of 20 October 1995

## Dear Sirs,

## I refer to the above letter.

On the basis of the information provided on submission of your agreement, the Commission's Directorate-General for Competition considers that this agreement appears to fulfil the conditions for exemption under Commission Regulation (EC) No 870/95 of 20 April 1995 (Official Journal of the European Communities No L/.89,p.7) instituting a block exemption for consortia agreements. Therefore no further action will be taken by the Commission in respect of the notified agreement provided the trade share is below 30%.

It is stated in the document submitted that the parties have recently decided to terminate the MEDSAP agreement which will expire on 31st December 1995. I would draw your attention to the provisions in Article 9 of Commission Regulation No 870/95; which states that any consortium enjoying the benefit of the Regulation must comply with the obligations stated therein. Those obligations must be strictly respected by MEDSAP consortium until 31st December 1995. Furthermore, I would therefore draw your attention to the conditions relating to trade share under Article 6 and of the obligation of the parties to notify their subsequent agreement if the trade share is above 30%.

Your agreement can therefore be dealt with by means of an administrative letter. Consequently the file will now be closed.

Yours sincerely,
