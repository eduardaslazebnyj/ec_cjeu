12.2.2.8. Application of the 10% turnover limit
(2240) The total amount of the fine for the Infringement does not exceed 10% of Alphabet’s
total turnover relating to the business year preceding the date of adoption of a
Commission decision pursuant to Article 23(2) of Regulation 1/2003. Therefore, no
further adjustment of the fine is required.
12.2.3. Joint and several liability
(2241) The Commission intends to hold all Alphabet legal entities mentioned in section 11.2
above jointly and severally liable for the Infringement. They should therefore be held
jointly and severally liable to pay the fine. Alphabet is jointly and severally liable for
the period from 2 October 2015, the date of its incorporation, until the end date of the
Infringement.
12.2.4. Conclusion: final amount of the fine
(2242) The Commission concludes that the final amount of the fine to be imposed on
Google amounts to EUR 2 954 291 000.
(2243) Google's turnover in the business year ending 31 December 2024 was EUR 323 382
million. As the amount of the fine set out in recital (2242) is below 10% of that
figure no adaptation is necessary pursuant to Article 23(2) of Regulation 1/2003.
12.3. Article 24(1)(a) of Regulation 1/2003 - periodic penalty payment
12.3.1. Principles
(2244) Pursuant to Article 24(1)(a) of Regulation 1/2003 and Article 5 of Council
Regulation (EC) No 2894/94, the Commission may, by decision, impose on
undertakings or associations of undertakings periodic penalty payments not
exceeding 5% of the average daily turnover in the preceding business year per day
and calculated from the day specified in the decision, in order to compel them to put
an end to an infringement, in accordance with a decision taken pursuant to Article 7
of Regulation 1/2003.
12.3.2. Application to this case
(2245) The Commission concludes that it is necessary to impose periodic penalty payments
pursuant to Article 24(1)(a) of Regulation 1/2003 and Article 5 of Council
Regulation (EC) No 2894/94 if Google fails to: (i) notify the Commission, within 60
days following notification of this Decision, of how it intends to discontinue the
practices identified in this Decision, in so far as it has not already done so; (iii) notify
the Commission, within 60 days following notification of this Decision, of the
measures by means of which it intends to effectively bring the Infringement to an
end; and (iii) implement, within 90 days following notification of this Decision or the
extended period (if relevant), the measures to effectively bring the Infringement to an
end.

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

Article 1
1. Google LLC and Alphabet Inc. have infringed Article 102 of the Treaty and Article 54 of
the EEA Agreement by participating in a single and continuous infringement consisting of
two infringements in their own right, namely: the Buy-side Conduct and the Sell-side
Conduct.
2. The single and continuous infringement was committed since the following dates: (a) 1
January 2014 as regards Google LLC and (b) 2 October 2015 as regards Alphabet Inc. The
single and continuous infringement is continuing as at the date of adoption of this Decision.
3. The geographical scope of the single and continuous infringement is (i) as regards the Buyside Conduct, the entire EEA from 1 January 2014 until the date of adoption of this Decision;
and (ii) as regards the Sell-side Conduct, the EEA with the exception of France from 1
January 2014 until 30 September 2020 and the entire EEA from 30 September 2020 until the
date of adoption of this Decision.

Article 2
For the single and continuous infringement consisting of two separate infringements referred to
in Article 1, a fine of EUR 2 954 291 000 is imposed on Google LLC, of which Alphabet Inc. is
jointly and severally liable for EUR 2 569 414 000.
The fine shall be credited, in euros, within three months of the date of notification of this
Decision, to the following bank account held in the name of the European Commission:

Article 3
1. Google LLC and Alphabet Inc. shall, within 60 days from the date of notification of this
Decision, notify the Commission how they intend to terminate the practices identified in this
Decision, in so far as they have not already done so, and of any measures by means of which
they intend to avoid the recurrence of such practices.
2. Google LLC and Alphabet Inc. shall, within 90 days from the date of notification of this
Decision, bring the single and continuous infringement effectively to an end by terminating the
practices identified in this Decision, in so far as they have not already done so, and by
implementing any measures required to avoid their recurrence.
3. The Commission may extend the period set out in subparagraph 2 above, on its own initiative
or upon request by Google LLC and/or Alphabet Inc., if necessary to enable the proper
assessment and implementation of certain defined measures required to avoid the recurrence of
the practices identified in this Decision.

incur a daily periodic penalty payment of 5% of the average daily turnover of the undertaking to
which they belong in the business year preceding such a failure to comply.
