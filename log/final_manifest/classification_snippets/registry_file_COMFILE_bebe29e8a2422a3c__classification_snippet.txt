Opinion of the Advisory Committee on restrictive agreements and dominant position given at its meeting of 1 September 2014 regarding a draft decision relating to Case AT.39574 — Smart Card Chips
Rapporteur: France
(2017/C 27/10)
1.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission that the anticompetitive behaviour covered by the draft decision constitutes concerted practices between undertakings within the meaning of Article 101 TFEU.
2.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission’s assessment of the product and geographic scope of the concerted practices.
3.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission that the undertakings concerned by the draft decision have participated in a single and continuous infringement concerning smart card chips within the meaning of Article 101 TFEU.
4.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission that the concerted practices have as its object the restriction of competition within the meaning of Article 101 TFEU.
5.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission that the concerted practices have been capable of appreciably affecting trade between the Member States of the EU.
6.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission’s assessment of the duration of the infringement.
7.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission draft decision as regards the addressees of the decision.
8.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission that a fine should be imposed on the addressees of the draft decision.
9.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission on the determination of the value of sales.
10.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission on the periods to be taken into account for imposing the fines.
11.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission on the basic amounts of the fines.
12.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission’s assessment on the mitigating circumstances applicable in this case.
13.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission on the reduction of fines based on the 2006 Leniency Notice.
14.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission on the reduction for the length of the proceedings. One Member State disagrees.
15.
The Advisory Committee agrees with the Commission on the final amounts of the fines.
16.
The Advisory Committee recommend the publication of its opinion in the
Official Journal of the European Union
.
