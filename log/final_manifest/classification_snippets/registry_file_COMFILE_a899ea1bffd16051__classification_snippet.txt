# Case Comp/A/38.477/D2

# British Airways/SN Brussels Airlines

## Commitments

SN Brussels Airlines (SN) and British Airways plc (BA) together submit the commitments specified below. These commitments are offered by SN and BA in order to remove any concerns as to the compatibility of the agreements notified in this case with Article 81(1) and/or 81(3) of the EC Treaty. The commitments are without prejudice to SN's and BA's arguments that the notified agreements do not infringe Article 81(1) or, in the alternative, fulfill the criteria for exemption set out in Article 81(3) of the EC Treaty.

-The commitments are subject to an exemption being granted pursuant to Article 5(3) of Council Regulation No. 3975/87 for a period of six years from 10 December 2002 and will take effect from the date the exemption becomes effective.

-It is acknowledged that a new entrant air carrier was able to obtain slots through the normal slot allocation process at Brussels National Airport (BRU) to operate a three frequency scheduled air service on a daily basis (for a minimum of five days a week) between BRU and Manchester (MAN) (a New Air Service) commencing in the summer 2003 IATA scheduling season although it has now postponed its entry on the BRU/MAN route.

\- For the duration of the exemption and provided no air carrier other than SN and BA is operating or has obtained slots to operate a New Air Service, SN and BA commit to making slots available at BRU without compensation to an air carrier (the Applicant) for the operation of a service from BRU to MAN. The number of slots to be made available is such number that together with the slots the Applicant has been able to obtain through the normal slot allocation purposes for the BRU/MAN service and any slots operated by other carriers on the BRU/MAN route (not including SN and BA), total three services daily (a minimum of five days a week) on the BRU/MAN route.

It is understood that if the parties make slots available to a new entrant to operate a New Air Service (3 daily frequencies) and a third carrier then enters the market with slots obtained through the normal procedure (and corresponding to one daily frequency for example), this provision cannot be interpreted as meaning that the first new entrant should return slots corresponding to one daily frequency to the parties, subject to the other provisions of this commitment.

It is furthermore understood that the commitments should not prevent a new entrant from increasing the number of frequencies it operates on the BRU/MAN route: if an Applicant obtains slots from the parties to operate a New Air Service (3 daily frequencies), and is subsequently able to add a fourth frequency, the

Applicant will not be obliged to return slots corresponding to one frequency to SN and BA, subject to the other provisions of this commitment.

\- The Applicant must have exhausted all reasonable efforts to obtain such slots through the normal slot allocation process including efforts for the allocation of slots by the BRU airport co-ordinator following the slot return deadline and rebalancing any slots it already has at BRU between services. The Applicant will not be deemed to have exhausted all reasonable efforts if slots were offered within 45 minutes of the times requested but not accepted by the Applicant and /or slots were offered more than 45 minutes from the times requested and the Applicant did not give SN and BA the opportunity to exchange those slots for slots within 45 minutes of the times requested.

-Should SN and BA be required to provide slots to the Applicant, they will provide slots within 45 minutes of the time requested by the Applicant as part of the normal slot allocation process (or if the parties do not have slots within 45 minutes of the time requested by the Applicant, they will provide the slot which is closest in time). SN and BA are not required to offer more than one slot within each of the morning period, noon/lunchtime period and evening period.

\- The Applicant must first notify SN and BA of its intention to apply for slots pursuant to these commitments at least six weeks prior to the IATA slot conference for the traffic season in which the Applicant intends to commence service (by fax or email with an acknowledgment of receipt to Mr E. Follet, fax: +32 2 723 8499, email: efollet@brussels-airlines.com). Should the Applicant then be unable to obtain slots through the normal slot allocation process, it must then notify SN and BA of its request for slots from them no more than two weeks following the end of that IATA slot conference. Within no more than two weeks after receipt of such notification from the applicant, the parties will then confirm in writing to the applicant the exact slots and the date by which they will be handed over.

\- The slots obtained from SN and BA may only be used by the Applicant to operate services on the route between BRU and MAN. If the Applicant does not operate the slots with sufficient regularity to maintain historic rights (currently the 80/20 rule) or if it does not use the slots on the BRU/MAN route, the slots must be returned to SN or BA as the case may be. In such case the obligation of SN and BA to make these or other slots available pursuant to these commitments continues. Should an Applicant to whom slots have been made available notify the parties too late in an IATA scheduling season for the parties to use the returned slots pursuant to the provisions of Regulation (EEC) 95/93 on the allocation of slots at Community airports (or any other Community legislation that amends or supersedes it), at any stage before the start of the IATA scheduling season, the parties may request the Applicant to transfer a comparable slot. If the

Applicant is unable to do so, it may seek to justify the non-use of the returned slot in accordance with the provisions of that Regulation.

\- The Applicant will be obliged to continue to try to obtain slots at the times originally requested (or closer to such times than the slots made available by the parties) in subsequent seasons through the normal slot allocation procedures. Should the Applicant obtain slots at the times originally requested (or at times closer to such times than the slots made available by the parties), the slots originally obtained from SN or BA will be returned to SN or BA.

-These commitments expire (and the Applicant must return slots to SN or BA) if the exemption terminates or otherwise ceases to apply or is annulled or revoked, the notified agreements are terminated or SN and BA stop co-operating or operating on the BRU/MAN route.

\- SN and BA may apply to the Commission for these commitments to be amended or revoked in the event that circumstances mean that this would be appropriate (for example, developments in the slot capacity situation at BRU, developments on the BRU/MAN route, or developments in services from and to neighbouring airports).
