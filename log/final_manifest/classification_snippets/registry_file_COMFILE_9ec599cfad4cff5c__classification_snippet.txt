## Subject: Case COMP/D2/37.982 Grand Alliance/Americana Consortium

This letter is addressed to you in your quality as representative of Hapag-Lloyd Aktiengesellschaft, Nippon Yusen Kaisha, Orient Overseas Container Line, P&O Nedlloyd, Lykes Lines Limited and Mexican Lines Limited.

I refer to the above application made on 10 October 2000 pursuant to Article 7 of Regulation 823/2000. The six months time limit for opposing the deemed exemption therefore expired on 11 April 2001.

The purpose of this letter is to inform you that the Commission did not oppose the deemed exemption of the agreement as notified (Memorandum of understanding and Operational Agreement) pursuant to Article 7 of Regulation 823/2000 on or before 11 April 2001. Accordingly, the notified agreement is deemed exempt for the duration of Regulation 823/2000, i.e. until 25 April 2005.

Yours sincerely,
